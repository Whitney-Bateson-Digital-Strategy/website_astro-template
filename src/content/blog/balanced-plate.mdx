Forget the math. Here's a practical, visual approach to building meals that nourish you without spending your evening entering food into an app.

## The half-plate rule

Start by filling half your plate with vegetables — not because vegetables are magic, but because they add volume, fiber, and nutrients without crowding out other foods. This isn't a rule to follow perfectly. It's a starting point.

## Protein and what it actually does

Protein keeps you full longer and stabilizes blood sugar, which means fewer energy crashes and less afternoon reaching-for-snacks behavior. It doesn't have to be meat — beans, eggs, Greek yogurt, and tofu all count.

## The part most people skip

Fat. Healthy fats (olive oil, avocado, nuts) help your body absorb fat-soluble nutrients and contribute to satiety. Low-fat diets often leave people hungry an hour later. Fat belongs on your plate.

The goal is a plate that keeps you satisfied for 3–4 hours without overthinking it.
