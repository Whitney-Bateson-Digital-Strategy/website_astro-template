Most diets fail not because you lack discipline, but because they're designed without your real life in mind. They assume consistent schedules, unlimited grocery budgets, and a brain that doesn't rebel when told it can't have something.

## The restriction trap

When you restrict a food, you make it more interesting. Your brain treats forbidden foods as rewards, which means the more you cut out, the louder those cravings get. This isn't a character flaw — it's basic psychology.

## What works instead

A sustainable approach to eating is built on flexibility, not rules. Instead of asking "is this allowed?", start asking "how does this make me feel?" That shift takes time, but it changes everything.

The goal isn't a perfect diet. It's a relationship with food that doesn't require constant willpower to maintain.
